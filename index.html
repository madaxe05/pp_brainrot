Privacy Policy

Brainrot Backrooms Night Shift · Last updated 29 October 2026

This policy describes information handled by Brainrot Backrooms Night Shift, an Android game by Madaxe Studios and Veritas Studios. Features can vary by app version and your choices.

Information saved on your device

The game stores progress, settings, rewards and gameplay records on your device. It does not require an account. Removing the app or clearing its data may delete this local information.

Advertising

Versions with advertising use Google AdMob to show app-open, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Google and its partners may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and operate their services. An internet connection is needed for ads. Rewarded ads are optional.

Where required, a consent message is presented before ads are requested. If a privacy-options control is required in your region, it is available in the app's settings. You may also manage or reset your Android advertising ID in your device settings. See How Google uses information for advertising and Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics and crash reports

Versions that enable Google Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics may process app events, device and app information, installation identifiers, diagnostic information and crash reports to understand usage and fix problems. Availability and collection depend on the version and applicable consent choices. Google's Firebase privacy information describes the information and retention periods for these services.

Sharing, security and retention

We use Google as a service provider for the features above; information may be processed in countries other than your own. Data sent by these services is protected in transit using their security measures. Local game data remains until you clear app data or uninstall. Google retains service data according to its published service policies and configuration; for example, Firebase describes its Crashlytics retention in the link above.

Your choices and requests

You can decline optional rewarded ads and manage applicable consent choices in the game when available. To ask about access, correction or deletion of information associated with the services, contact us. Please do not include sensitive information in an initial message; we may need information to identify the relevant data and may be unable to identify information not linked to an account.

Children

This horror game is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us so we can investigate.
